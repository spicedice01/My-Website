Our Mission

Building a Brighter
Future Together

We believe in the power of community-driven development to create lasting, positive change for generations to come. Every initiative is designed with and for the communities we serve.

Founded by Dr. Sammy Gitonga, the foundation operates across Nakuru County and beyond, bringing dignity, opportunity, and hope to thousands of families every year.

Education First

Providing access to quality education and vocational training to break the cycle of poverty.

Healthcare Access

Delivering essential medical services and health education to remote communities.

Economic Growth

Supporting local entrepreneurs and sustainable business development through micro-finance.

The Foundation

Rooted in community.
Built for lasting change.

The Sammy Gitonga Foundation brings people, practical action, and shared purpose together to help communities shape a more hopeful future.

Founded by Dr. Sammy Gitonga, our work begins with listening. We partner with communities across Nakuru County and beyond to turn local insight into opportunity, dignity, and measurable progress.

Our Mission

Create opportunity where it matters most.

To create sustainable change through education, healthcare, economic empowerment, and community-led action.

Our Vision

Communities leading their own future.

A Kenya where every person can learn, thrive, participate, and build a dignified life.

Where We Work

Nakuru and beyond.

Our roots are in Nakuru County, with partnerships and outreach shaped by the needs of the communities we serve.

How We’re Led

People, accountability, purpose.

Founder-led with community voices at the center, our governance approach is grounded in stewardship, transparency, and responsible partnerships.
